Our Story

Key milestones since day one.

2018Born during the COVID‑19 pandemic as a side project to help people earn from home. Initially a simple survey tool, it quickly evolved into a micro‑task platform.
2019First prototype launched with 100 testers. Valuable feedback led to a complete redesign of the user interface and reward mechanics.
2020Pivoted to a micro‑review model after user feedback. Introduced gamification elements to boost engagement.
2021Reached 10,000 active users and introduced the reward system. Partnered with early brands for product testing campaigns.
2022Partnership with major brands for exclusive product testing. Launched the referral program, growing the user base exponentially.
2023Global expansion & new reward system (USDC payouts). Opened offices in Europe and Asia.
2024Launched the beta version, 10k users. Introduced AI‑powered content moderation for review quality.
2025AI‑powered review quality checks introduced. Achieved profitability and started paying out over $1M in rewards.
2026TaskFlow becomes a benchmark in micro‑tasking. Over 50,000 active users and partnerships with 200+ brands.
